Governance & Phase-1 Scope Cut

  • No standalone hashtags.parquet or ~/.jabs/hashtags_ledger.jsonl in Phase 1: Avoids stale caches after git pull, jabs-cli merge, prune, or update-pose.
  • Vocab lives in project.json["hashtag_vocabulary"]: Syncs automatically with Hub projects.settings; excluded from jabs/annotations/*.json globs used by update-labels.
